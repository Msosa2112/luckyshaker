Nose: Toasted caramel, bourbon oak char, rich cream, and delicate hints of dark cocoa nibs.

Palate: Silky and dense with an initial burst of vanilla bean warmth, giving way to spiced oak whiskey character and sweet cream.

Finish: Long, warming, and comforting with lingering chocolate and toasted malt.

  1. Chill: Place bottle in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours before serving.
  2. Shake: Shake vigorously for 10 seconds to awaken the velvety cream emulsion.
  3. Pour: Serve 3.5 oz over a single large clear ice sphere in an old-fashioned tumbler.
  4. Garnish: Optional freshly grated nutmeg or dark chocolate shavings.
