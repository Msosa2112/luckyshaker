SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY

Last Updated: September 2026 • Direct-to-Consumer Alcohol Fulfillment Standards

MANDATORY 21+ ADULT SIGNATURE: Under federal and state alcohol control laws, all shipments containing distilled spirits and bottled cocktails require the physical signature of an adult aged 21 or older with valid government-issued photo ID upon carrier delivery. Packages cannot be left unattended on doorsteps.

1. Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Alcohol Shipping Laws

Lucky Shaker Bartender LLC operates in strict compliance with applicable federal, state, and municipal regulations governing the sale and direct shipment of alcoholic beverages. All sales are solicited, accepted, and processed through our licensed beverage fulfillment partner facilities. Title to and ownership of all alcoholic beverages passes from the seller to the buyer in the state where the order is fulfilled.

2. Permitted Delivery States

Due to varying direct-to-consumer alcohol interstate commerce statutes, we currently ship directly to consumers in the majority of US states where statutory reciprocity permits distilled spirit shipments. We do not ship to P.O. Boxes, APO/FPO addresses, or jurisdictions designated as dry counties. If your state or ZIP code is currently restricted, our checkout portal will notify you prior to charge authorization.

3. Temperature-Stable Packaging & Climate Holds

Our bottled cocktails are crafted with genuine fresh dairy cream and delicate botanical oils. To ensure lounge-grade freshness year-round:

  • All bottles are nestled inside custom-molded, recycled thermal pulp insulation engineered for shock absorption and temperature moderation.
  • During extreme heatwaves (above 95°F / 35°C) or deep winter freezes (below 20°F / -6°C), orders may be placed on a temporary Weather Hold until regional weather windows guarantee safe transit.

4. Processing, Fulfillment & Transit Times

Orders are hand-inspected and packed within 1–2 business days (Monday through Friday). Courier transit times typically range from 2 to 5 business days depending on destination proximity to our Louisville, KY fulfillment atelier. You will receive an automated tracking link with delivery estimates once your order departs the facility.

5. Carrier Attempts & Failed Deliveries

Carriers will make up to three (3) consecutive delivery attempts before returning the parcel to our facility. If an order is returned due to absence of an adult of legal drinking age to sign, restocking fees and secondary shipping carrier fees will apply to re-shipment requests.

6. Contact Regarding Shipments

For inquiries regarding tracking, transit rerouting, or scheduled deliveries, please contact concierge@luckyshaker.com.
