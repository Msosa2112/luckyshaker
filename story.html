COCKTAILS, WITHOUT THE BAR

Born from the conviction that world-class mixology should never be confined to a bar counter or held hostage by complicated recipes.

"We spent three years obsessed with one question: Can a bottled cocktail deliver the exact aromatics, silky mouthfeel, and spirited punch of a $25 drink served in the world’s finest hotel lounges?"

The answer required tossing aside industrial shortcuts. No artificial emulsifiers, no high-fructose corn syrups, and no synthetic colorings. Every bottle of Lucky Shaker is hand-crafted with barrel-aged distillates, farm-fresh dairy cream, cold-extracted citrus oils, and raw botanicals.

— KATHERIN • MASTER MIXOLOGIST & FOUNDER
THE CRAFT PHILOSOPHY

UNCOMPROMISED SPIRITS, SCIENTIFIC PRECISION.

Cocktail balance is chemistry. The interaction between spirit ethanol, dairy fats, citrus acidity, and natural sugars requires exacting temperature calibration during formulation. By chilling our blends to near-freezing temperatures prior to nitrogen-flushed bottling, we lock in delicate botanical top notes that would otherwise oxidize.

When you shake a Lucky Shaker bottle at home, you aren’t just mixing liquid—you are aerating the spirit emulsion, activating the aromatic citrus oils, and producing the signature velvety micro-foam that defines true craft hospitality.

OUR THREE-PILLAR METHOD

FROM BARREL TO BOTTLE

01

AGED HERITAGE DISTILLATES

We partner exclusively with legacy distillers. From charred-oak Kentucky straight bourbon to triple-distilled whiskey and pure column-distilled Caribbean white rum.

02

COLD-EXTRACTED BOTANICALS

Our aromatic bitters, Tahitian lime zests, and spearmint leaves undergo gentle cold extraction to preserve lively essential oils without thermal degradation.

03

NITROGEN SEALED IN GLASS

Bottled in heavy-weight apothecary flint glass and sealed under inert nitrogen to ensure peak flavor stability for up to 12 months on your home bar cart.
