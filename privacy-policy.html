PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 2026 • Your Data Privacy & Security Standards

1. Overview & Commitment

Lucky Shaker Bartender LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to safeguarding personal information collected through our website, mobile interfaces, and digital ordering services. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we gather, how it is processed, and your privacy rights under applicable data protection laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

When you browse or interact with our storefront, we collect:

  • Order & Contact Details: Name, billing and shipping address, email address, and telephone number.
  • Age Verification Information: Date of birth and self-attestation confirming you are 21 years of age or older prior to entering and completing alcohol transactions.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type, referring pages, time zone, and interaction cookies to optimize 60fps canvas performance and cart persistence.

3. Payment Security & PCI-DSS Compliance

We do not store, process, or retain raw payment card account numbers on our servers. All credit card, debit card, Apple Pay, and Shop Pay transactions are encrypted and transmitted directly to PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways with 256-bit SSL encryption.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We disclose information solely to authorized third parties strictly necessary to deliver our services, including:

  • Licensed alcoholic beverage fulfillment partners and warehouses.
  • Insured common couriers (e.g. FedEx, UPS) to verify adult signature and complete parcel transit.
  • Fraud prevention, age verification, and regulatory compliance authorities when required by statute.

5. Your Rights & Contact

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. To exercise these rights or opt out of promotional communications, email our Data Privacy Officer at privacy@luckyshaker.com.
