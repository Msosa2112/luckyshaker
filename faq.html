FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our bottled cocktails, shipping regulations, shelf stability, and serving guidelines.

01 // SHELF LIFE, STORAGE & INGREDIENTS

Unopened bottles have an optimal shelf stability of 12 months stored at room temperature away from direct sunlight. Because we flush each bottle with inert nitrogen prior to capping, the delicate botanical top notes and spirit character remain preserved as freshly bottled.
Before opening, refrigeration is not required (though we highly recommend chilling before serving). Once opened, please refrigerate and enjoy within 6 months.
Absolutely none. We do not use high-fructose corn syrup, artificial coloring, or synthetic emulsifiers. We rely on authentic spirits, natural citrus oils, real sugar cane, and fresh cream.

02 // SHIPPING, COURIERS & 21+ VERIFICATION

Yes. Federal and state law requires an adult signature (21+ years of age) with valid government-issued photo ID for all alcohol shipments. Packages cannot be left unattended on doorsteps or delivered to P.O. Boxes.
Every order is packed in custom molded thermal pulp pods engineered to cushion heavy apothecary glass while shielding contents from extreme ambient temperature fluctuations during transit.
We back every parcel with our 100% Bottle Breakage Guarantee. If your shipment incurs any damage in transit, simply send a photo to concierge@luckyshaker.com within 48 hours of delivery and we will ship an expedited replacement immediately.

03 // SERVING & MIXOLOGY RECOMMENDATIONS

Each 750ml bottle provides approximately 6 to 8 full-sized craft cocktail pours (3.2 oz to 4 oz per pour), depending on the drink and glassware used.
No additions are necessary. Lucky Shaker is completely pre-formulated and ready to pour. You only need ice. Fresh garnishes (such as an orange peel for the Old Fashioned or a mint sprig for the Mojito) can be added to elevate the visual presentation.
